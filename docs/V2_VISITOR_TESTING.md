# First-time visitor evaluation — ready-to-run guide

Status: prepared; **no participants recruited or tested** in this implementation.
Agent critique, automated browser traversal and genuine human research must be
reported separately. The owner can run this with approximately five people who
have not seen the project. Do not contact anyone or record them without permission.

## Setup

Use the final local candidate or a separately authorized preview. Record its
commit, URL, browser, OS, screen size, input method and reduced-motion preference.
Try a mix of desktop and real touch devices. Do not explain the thesis first.
Ask permission for observation and any screen/audio recording. Names and
identifying details are unnecessary; use participant codes P01–P05.

## Opening prompt

“Please explore this as you normally would. You can stop whenever you feel
finished. Let me know if anything feels confusing or frustrating.”

Allow 2–4 minutes without coaching. Record where the person scrolls, reverses,
pauses, uses chapters, misses an instruction or leaves. If they ask for help,
record the request before responding. Do not infer a cause from silence alone.

## Follow-up questions

1. What do you think this is about?
2. Which transition do you remember most clearly?
3. Did any moment feel confusing, cramped, hard to read or unresponsive?
4. What did you understand from the ending?
5. Was there anything you wanted to inspect but could not reach?

Then, if relevant, ask whether they noticed the same photograph across the acts
and what changed in the anticipation scene. Keep these prompted answers separate
from spontaneous recognition. Offer the reading/skip path afterward and ask
whether it supplies the information they needed.

## Owner's outstanding validation checklist

- [ ] Genuine first-time visitors observed; observations separated from interpretation.
- [ ] Real iPhone/Safari and Android touch traversal, reversal, orientation and browser-bar changes.
- [ ] Physical wheel and trackpad traversal with small/large deltas and pinch/zoom.
- [ ] Physical Safari keyboard/focus, reduced motion and CSS 3D inspected.
- [ ] Screen reader tested on target OS/browser with skip, chapters and reading room.
- [ ] Representative lower-end device reviewed for heat, input response and visible stutter.
- [ ] Latest dependency audit and final public-origin behavior checked before publication.
- [ ] Explicit push/merge/deployment/release approval obtained.

Use observations to prioritize comprehension and pacing changes. A small
qualitative sample does not establish learning outcomes or population metrics.
